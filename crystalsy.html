The Craft

Carved, not cast

Each Shivling begins as a rough block of natural crystal, selected by hand for clarity. Our artisans shape it in stages — rough-cutting the form, then rounding and polishing by hand until the surface takes on a smooth, glass-like finish.

No two pieces are identical. Natural inclusions, faint striations, and subtle tone variation are part of the stone, not a flaw — they're what tells you it's crystal, not resin.

Care Guide

Keeping it at its best

  • Wipe with a soft, dry or slightly damp cloth — avoid harsh chemical cleaners.
  • Keep out of direct, prolonged sunlight to preserve surface clarity.
  • Handle by the base when lifting to avoid pressure on the crystal form.
  • Store in the padded box provided if not on permanent display.
