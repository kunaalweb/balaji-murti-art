The Atelier Legacy

Generations of Devotion, Chiseled to Perfection

Rooted in Jaipur's centuries-old lapidary heritage, Balaji Murti Art & Gems shapes rare natural crystals, quartz, and semi-precious minerals into revered deities and sacred energy forms for home mandirs and spiritual sanctuaries worldwide.

Every piece — from an intricate Sphatik Shivling and Green Jade Ganesh to a hand-strung healing bracelet — is carved from solid earth-born stone. Free from synthetic resins, moulds, or factory mass-production, each creation is an enduring tribute to divine grace.
