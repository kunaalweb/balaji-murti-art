Our Craft

Rooted in tradition, cut for today

Balaji Murti Art & Gems began as a small family workshop working with natural crystal and semi-precious stone, shaping Ganesh, Shivling, and Shree Yantra murtis for home temples across India.

Every piece — from a hand-carved crystal murti to a beaded gemstone bracelet — passes through the same hands that select, cut, and polish it. Nothing is rushed, and nothing leaves the workshop that we wouldn't keep for ourselves.
